# 🐔 The Flock of Hope: Rural Chicken Distribution

Based on Bill Gates's brilliant insight that "it's the most straightforward way to end hunger and poverty."

## How It Works

### The Starter Package
Each family receives:
- 20-25 young hens + 1 rooster
- 2 months of starter feed
- Basic veterinary supplies
- Coop building instructions

### The "Passing on the Gift" Model
- Families pledge to give their first 10 chicks to another family
- Creates exponential community growth
- Builds social cohesion and trust

### Economic Impact
- Daily eggs for nutrition
- Surplus eggs/chickens for market sale
- Sustainable income generation
- Women's economic empowerment
