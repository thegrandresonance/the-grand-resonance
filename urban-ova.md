# 🥚 Urban Ova Initiative: Ethical Suburban Egg Farming

## Modern Solution for Urban Nutrition

### Farm Locations
- Medium-scale farms in suburban areas
- Close to urban centers for distribution
- Ethical, cage-free environments

### No-Slaughter Policy
- Older hens are retired, not slaughtered
- Designated sanctuary areas
- Adoption programs for retired hens

### Distribution System
- **30% Free**: Distributed to schools, shelters, food banks
- **70% Subsidized**: Sold below market rate to communities
- Profits reinvested into expanding the program

### Community Benefits
- Local employment opportunities
- Educational programs for schools
- Sustainable urban food security
